# Privacy Policy - Tic-Tac-Toe

**Last updated:** September 9, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the Tic-Tac-Toe application collects, uses, and protects your information.

## Commitment to Privacy

Your right to privacy is fundamental to us. This application has been developed with a focus on protecting user privacy.

## Information We DO NOT Collect

This application **DOES NOT collect any personal information**, including:
- Name, email, phone number, or other identifying information
- Geographic location
- Device information (IMEI, Android ID, etc.)
- Contacts, photos, or other personal files
- Browsing or usage data
- Third-party information

## Information Stored Locally

The application stores only the following information **locally on your device**:

### Game Settings:
- Sound preferences (on/off)
- Vibration preferences (on/off)
- Preferred theme (System/Light/Dark)
- Default AI difficulty
- Preferred starting player
- Tutorial completion status

### Anonymous Statistics:
- Total games played
- Win/loss/draw counts by game mode
- Win streaks (current and best)
- Current game state (to resume games)

**Important:** All this information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

## Internet Connection

This application **DOES NOT connect to the internet** and **DOES NOT transmit data** to external servers, analytics companies, or third-party services.

## App Permissions

The application requests only one permission:
- **VIBRATE:** Used to provide tactile feedback during gameplay when the option is enabled

## Third-Party Services

This application **DOES NOT use**:
- Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
- Advertising networks
- Tracking services
- Automatic crash reporting
- Social networks or login services

## Data Backup

App settings and statistics may be included in your device's standard Android backup. These backups are managed by the Android system and your Google account settings, not by this application.

## Children's Data Protection

This application is safe for children of all ages. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information from users of any age.

## Changes to This Policy

If future changes are made to this Privacy Policy, the "Last updated" date will be revised. We recommend checking this policy periodically to be aware of any changes.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.

---

**Executive Summary:** This is an offline game that does not collect personal data, does not connect to the internet, and stores only basic settings and statistics locally on your device.
